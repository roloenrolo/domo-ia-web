02

How it gets installed

The engine is the same; what changes is the configuration for your business.

STEP 1

Assessment

We measure your real funnel: how many leads arrive, how many you answer and how many end up as meetings. Without that number, there is no promise.

STEP 2

Installation

Antonia goes into your WhatsApp with your way of speaking and your qualification rules. You approve how she answers before she handles anything on her own.

STEP 3

Weekly measurement

Every week you get the funnel measured: what came in, what got booked and what to improve. What isn't measured isn't charged for, and isn't promised.

Purpose

Let nothing slip away.

Domo IA exists so that no sales opportunity is lost to disorder, delay or lack of follow-up, combining artificial intelligence and human judgment to grow businesses with more clarity and confidence.

What we are building

Digital teams tailored for Chilean SMEs that receive leads through WhatsApp.

We combine campaigns, customer service, qualification, follow-up, auditing and automations connected to their tools, without replacing the human team.

Where we are going

We imagine a world where every inquiry gets a timely answer, every opportunity has a clear next step and every sales team can focus on the conversations that really matter.

Companies stop chasing scattered data and start working with a living view of their funnel, while artificial intelligence handles the repetitive work with judgment, traceability and respect for people.

In that future, selling better does not mean pushing harder: it means listening better, answering with context and supporting every decision with confidence.

Domo IA will be the intelligent layer that connects a prospect's intent with the human action able to turn it into a lasting relationship.
