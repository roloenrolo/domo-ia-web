03 · The team

Nine. With names, roles and shifts.

Antonia

On duty

Receptionist · 24/7

Answers your WhatsApp in under a minute, qualifies leads and books meetings.

  • Answers new WhatsApp messages in under a minute, at any hour, any day.
  • Asks the qualifying questions you define — budget, income, timing, location — without sounding like a form.
  • Books the meeting straight into the right person's calendar, with the lead's details attached.
  • Follows up the lead that went lukewarm instead of letting the conversation die.
  • Hands over to a human the cases you flagged as off-limits for her to handle alone.

Matías

Ad analyst · 06:15

Shifts the budget to ads that bring conversations.

  • Reviews every morning how each ad performed: spend, leads, conversations and meetings.
  • Moves the budget toward ads that bring leads who reply, not ads that bring cheap clicks.
  • Tells you what is burning money, with the figure in plain sight, and pauses it the moment you approve.
  • Decides what to test the following week and commissions the creative from Sofía.
  • Works with Clara: she flags which ad brings messages but no meetings, and he fixes the campaign with that.
  • Leaves you a weekly report in plain language: what came in, what it cost and what's next.

Clara

Quality auditor · 08:30

Reviews every conversation and flags what got stuck.

  • Spots the week's trends: which new questions are coming in and which answers are working.
  • Finds the conversations that were about to book and didn't.
  • Turns them into concrete improvements: which sentence to change, which question to add, which case to escalate sooner.
  • Works with Matías: she flags which ad brings plenty of messages but almost no meetings, so he stops spending there.
  • Leaves the improvements ready for you to approve, and the following week measures whether they brought more meetings.

Sofía

Ad creative · weekly

Produces your ad creative with your brand.

  • Produces the creative for your ads — image, short video and copy — in your brand.
  • Builds variants of the same message so you can test angle, hook and call to action.
  • Keeps the creative bank alive so Matías always has something new to test.
  • Reuses what already worked instead of starting from scratch every month.

Amanda

Meeting notes · same day

Keeps an organized record of every sales meeting.

  • Takes notes on every sales meeting and leaves the summary tidy the same day.
  • Records what was agreed, the objections that came up and the next step committed to.
  • Leaves the record where your team can find it, without depending on the salesperson's memory.

Lucas

Evaluator · per meeting

Evaluates and scores every meeting with a rubric.

  • Grades every meeting against a professional sales rubric and gives it a score.
  • Shows what was done well and what was missing: discovery, objection handling, closing.
  • Compares meetings against each other so you can see who needs support and with what.

Laura

Sales coach · from 15 meetings

Trains your salespeople using your own meetings.

  • Gathers the patterns from every graded meeting and extracts what actually closes in your industry.
  • Trains your salespeople with examples from your own operation, not with theory.
  • Builds the conversation guide your team can use tomorrow.

Diego

Automation engineer

Turns a request in plain language into a working workflow.

  • Takes your request in plain words and turns it into a workflow that works.
  • Connects the tools you already use: WhatsApp, spreadsheets, email, your CRM.
  • Every request starts from a clear criterion: what should happen and how you know it worked.
  • Delivers the automation tested, never half-finished.

Sebastián

Quality control

Tests every automation before it reaches you.

  • Reviews every delivery from Diego with his own judgement: he takes nothing for granted.
  • Runs real tests with sample data before approving.
  • Rejects anything that doesn't meet what was agreed, even if it looks fine at first glance.
  • Nothing ships without his sign-off.

08

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us before booking the assessment.

What exactly is a digital worker?

An artificial intelligence agent with a name, a job title and one task it does well. It isn't software you have to learn to use: you hire it the way you hire a person, it starts working inside your operation, and it reports on what it did. Antonia answers WhatsApp, Matías moves the ad budget, Lucas grades the sales meetings.

How is this different from a chatbot?

A chatbot follows a decision tree: the moment the customer steps off the script, it breaks. An AI agent understands what it's being told, qualifies the lead against your criteria, checks your calendar and books. The practical difference is simple: the chatbot hands off to a human, the agent closes the meeting.

Is this for small companies or only for large ones?

It is built precisely for small and mid-sized companies that spend on advertising and receive leads over WhatsApp. Size isn't the test: the test is whether you're paying for leads that nobody answers in time. If that number turns out to be zero, we tell you so in the assessment and we don't sell you anything.

Which industries does it work for?

For any business that pays for leads and handles them over WhatsApp. The pattern repeats itself in real estate agencies and brokerages, insurance, clinics and dental practices, education and training, automotive, construction and professional services: a flood of leads arriving from ads or listing portals, all asking the same thing, and a response delay that decides whether the prospect stays with you or walks over to the company next door. We started in real estate because that's where the problem shows up most sharply, but the team doesn't belong to one industry: what changes from one sector to the next isn't the digital workers, it's the qualification rules and the way they speak — and that gets configured during the assessment.

How long does it take to answer a lead?

Under a minute — day, night, weekend and public holiday. That isn't a marketing promise: it's what gets measured every week in the funnel report you receive.

What happens to my customers' data, and to Chile's Law 21.719?

The data of the people who write to your agent is yours, not ours: we process it on your behalf, under contract, and only on your instructions. Chile's Law 21.719 takes effect on 1 December 2026, and we are bringing our processing records, contracts and retention periods into line with it before that date. It's all written out in the privacy policy.

What do I need in order to start?

A WhatsApp line where the leads land, and a willingness to show us your real numbers. The assessment measures how many leads come in, how many you answer and how many end up as meetings. Without that number, no promise is worth anything.

Is Domo IA related to Domo, Inc. or to DomoAI?

No. Domo IA is a Chilean company — DOMO IA SpA, Santiago — that installs AI agents inside sales teams. Domo, Inc. (domo.com) is a US business intelligence platform, and DomoAI (domoai.app) is a video generation tool. Similar names, different businesses.
